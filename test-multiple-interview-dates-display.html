Key Improvements

  • ✅ Interview Dates Summary: All interview dates are now displayed prominently at the top (highlighted in yellow)
  • ✅ Quick Overview: Users can immediately see all the dates when interviews were conducted
  • ✅ Date Range: Easy to identify the time span covered by the merged assessment
  • ✅ Duplicate Removal: If multiple interviews have the same date, it's shown only once
  • ✅ Detailed View: Each source interview still shows its individual interview date for traceability

Use Cases

  • Time Range Analysis: Quickly see when the interviews were conducted (e.g., "All interviews from Jan 10-22, 2024")
  • Historical Context: Understand if the data is recent or spans a longer period
  • Report Generation: Include the interview dates in reports without drilling into each source
  • Quality Assurance: Verify that all expected interviews from a specific timeframe are included
